## What this proves

This test proves the POC's hosted path can baseline `r/cats` and `r/dogs`, detect
a later eligible post, create a native attributed crosspost in the installed
destination, avoid a duplicate on the next poll, and stop cleanly.

It does not prove hundreds or thousands of sources, guaranteed real-time
delivery, deletion reconciliation, public distribution approval, or the later
push-notification and granular-filter app. Those remain production work and
require Reddit review plus a different scaling architecture.
